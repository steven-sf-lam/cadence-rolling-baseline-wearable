2. Data That May Be Collected

Only data necessary for analytics, research, and application functionality will be accessed.

3. How Data Is Used

Collected data may be used for the following purposes:

  • Running and wearable data analytics

4. Research Use

Data may be used in independent research related to wearable running analytics. When research findings are shared publicly, data will be aggregated, anonymized, or de-identified where reasonably possible.

Personal data will not be sold or used for advertising brokerage.

5. Data Sharing

Personal data will not be sold to third parties. Data may only be shared in the following limited situations:

6. Data Retention

Data will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for research, analytics, development, or security purposes. Data that is no longer required may be deleted or anonymized.
